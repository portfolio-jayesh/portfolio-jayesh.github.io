## Research question

How can small, reliable metallic interconnects connect a qubit chip and a separate readout/control chip without undermining the electrical requirements of superconducting quantum hardware?

## My work

- Developing **electroplated indium micro-bumps** for flip-chip and 3D integration.
- Working through **optical lithography, sputtering, wet/dry etching and electrodeposition** steps in cleanroom-compatible workflows.
- Investigating the roles of seed/under-bump layers, surface conditions and deposition uniformity.
- Studying related superconducting thin-film and microwave-device requirements.

## Physical idea

Indium provides a soft, superconducting metal connection at cryogenic temperatures. But an interconnect is only useful if its geometry, interface chemistry and processing are compatible with the chips and the surrounding circuit. Good bump growth alone does **not** establish low microwave loss or reliable cryogenic performance.
