# IZO/Ag/IZO multilayer transparent electrodes for tandem solar cells

**MSc Physics thesis · NCPRE, IIT Bombay · 2025–2026**  
**Research guide:** Dr. Dinesh Kabra

[← Research index](../../README.md) · **[Original thesis PDF](https://github.com/portfolio-jayesh/JayeshJa/blob/main/Assets/ThesisReport.pdf)** · [Project overview](https://github.com/portfolio-jayesh/JayeshJa/blob/main/thesis-report.html)

## Research question

Can an ultrathin silver layer reduce sheet resistance while the surrounding transparent oxides are tuned to preserve or improve light transmission?

## Approach

- Simulated **IZO / Ag / IZO** oxide–metal–oxide electrodes using **SETFOS**, multilayer thin-film optics and Python analysis.
- Varied individual nanolayer thicknesses and compared optical transmission across relevant wavelength ranges.
- Evaluated solar-spectrum-weighted photon transmission and **theoretical photocurrent upper limits**; these are *not measured device* `Jsc` values.
- Considered sub-10 nm silver optical losses, thin-film interference, sheet resistance and film-continuity limitations.
- Gained deposition/characterization experience including RF magnetron sputtering, thermal evaporation, UV–Vis–NIR spectroscopy, four-point probe measurements and profilometry.

## Selected modelled comparison

| Simulated structure | Mean transmission, 300–800 nm |
| --- | ---: |
| IZO, 140 nm | 78.7% |
| IZO / Ag / IZO, 45 / 8 / 60 nm | 85.2% |

The modelled gain is **6.5 percentage points** (about **8.3% relative**). The numbers describe an optical simulation under the stated wavelength window, **not an experimentally certified device-efficiency gain**.

## Why the material details matter

An 8 nm nominal Ag layer may be discontinuous because of island growth. Roughness, interface mixing and deposition conditions can change both optical properties and electrical performance. The full thesis discusses modelling assumptions and experimental considerations.
