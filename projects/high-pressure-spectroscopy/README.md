## Research question

What happens to the lattice vibrations and crystal structure of **Na₂Cu₂TeO₆** when it is compressed?

## Experimental exposure

- Conducted pressure-dependent **Raman spectroscopy** using a **diamond-anvil cell (DAC)** and optical spectroscopy setup.
- Investigated changes in vibrational features as pressure increased.
- Participated in a team experimental visit to **INDUS-2**, RRCAT, Indore, with high-pressure X-ray diffraction work at **Beamline 11**.
- Gained experience in experimental preparation, optical alignment, measurement interpretation and synchrotron facility procedures.

## Interpretation caveat

Changes or emerging features in Raman spectra can **suggest** structural changes, but do not by themselves establish a phase transition. Structural confirmation requires additional evidence, such as diffraction.
