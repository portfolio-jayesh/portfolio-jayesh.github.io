# PANI/Fe₃O₄ magnetic-polymer composite for EMI shielding

**BSc Honours research project · Lovely Professional University · 2022–2023**

[← Research index](../../README.md) · [Research CV](https://github.com/portfolio-jayesh/portfolio-jayesh.github.io/blob/main/assets/Jayesh_Jadhav_Resume.pdf)

## Aim

Explore an electrically and magnetically active composite material for **electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding**.

## My contribution

- Synthesized **Fe₃O₄ (magnetite)** and an *in-situ* polyaniline (**PANI**) composite.
- Used or participated in characterization with **FESEM, XRD and FTIR** for microstructure and material interpretation.
- Examined shielding behavior using a **vector network analyzer (VNA)** and collaborated on analysis of microwave measurements.

## Physics connection

EMI shielding depends on electromagnetic reflection, absorption and multiple-scattering mechanisms. A polymer–magnetic particle composite allows electrical and magnetic properties to contribute to microwave response.
